Maryland / Baltimore tenant rights
Quick reference for canvassers. All rights are specific to Maryland Real Property Article and Baltimore City code.
Failure to pay rent (most common case type)
✓
Right of redemption — Tenant can pay ALL owed rent + court fees before judgment to stop the eviction. This can even happen at the courthouse on hearing day. (Md. Real Property § 8-401)
✓
Right to appear — Tenant can attend and defend: raise habitability issues, contest the amount owed, or present evidence of payment.
✓
4-business-day buffer — After a judgment, the landlord must wait at least 4 business days before filing a warrant of restitution. This is time to find assistance.
✓
14-day eviction notice — In Baltimore City specifically, landlord must mail eviction notice 14 days before the physical eviction date (stricter than state law).
All tenants
✓
Rent escrow — If the landlord fails to maintain habitable conditions, tenant can pay rent into a court escrow account instead of to the landlord. (§ 8-211)
✓
Retaliation protection — Landlord cannot evict in retaliation for complaining to housing authorities, withholding rent legally, or organizing with other tenants. (§ 8-208.1)
✓
Habitability standard — Landlord must maintain functioning heat, plumbing, and freedom from pests. Violations are an affirmative defense in eviction cases.
✓
Free legal help — Maryland Legal Aid (410-539-5340), District Court Help Center (410-260-1392), and volunteer lawyers are available at Wabash every weekday morning.
