Maryland tenant rights reference
Quick reference for canvassers. All statutes cite Maryland Real Property Article.
Failure to Pay Rent (most common)
✓
Right of Redemption — Pay all rent owed + court fees before judgment to stop the eviction — even on hearing day. (§ 8-401)
✓
Right to Appear & Defend — Show up. Raise habitability issues, contest the amount, present payment proof.
✓
4-Business-Day Buffer — After judgment, landlord must wait 4 business days before filing a warrant of restitution.
✓
Emergency Rental Assistance — Call 211 immediately. A pending ERAP application can delay the hearing.
All tenants
✓
Rent Escrow — If landlord fails to maintain habitable conditions, you may pay rent into court escrow. (§ 8-211)
✓
Retaliation Protection — Cannot be evicted for complaining to housing authorities or organizing with neighbors. (§ 8-208.1)
✓
Habitability Standard — Landlord must maintain heat, plumbing, and pest-free conditions. Violations are a defense.
✓
Free Legal Help — Maryland Legal Aid (410-539-5340) and Public Justice Center are at Wabash courthouse weekday mornings.
